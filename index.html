A NAME WITH A LITTLE HISTORY

Small coin.
Lasting idea.

Our name begins with Fanam: a small gold or silver coin historically used in southern India. Museum collections preserve examples from Travancore, part of the region’s rich monetary history.

Fanio brings that inspiration into the present. A place to pay attention to the small amounts, make room for your plans, and see everyday money more clearly.

Is it available on Google Play?+

Google Play — coming soon. For now, this page offers Android APK requests. There is no announced store release date here.

Does Fanio connect to my bank?+

Fanio tracks the records you add; it does not directly connect to your bank. Available amounts are estimates from your income plan and, when confirmed, recorded account balances. They are not live bank balances.

Are transactions captured automatically?+

Manual tracking is available. Optional Android capture can read supported payment messages or notifications in builds where it is enabled, with your permission and a review workflow. It is disabled by default in the supplied app version; ask which features are enabled when requesting your APK.

Can I use it on an iPhone?+

This early-access page offers the Android APK. An iPhone download and release date are not available here.

Can I sign in on this website?+

This is Fanio’s introduction website. It shows example screens and helps you request the app. Your account and financial records stay in the separate Fanio application.
